Working out your patterns (a few seconds the first time)…

Click a piece, then its square. A wrong move shows their best reply and what it costs; try again from the start, or show the answer. Then step through the lines with ◀ ▶ (or the arrow keys) and play any move on the board to see what the engine answers. Scores are for you: +1.0 is a pawn up, #3 is mate in 3, #-3 is mate against you; grey moves are the engine's.
